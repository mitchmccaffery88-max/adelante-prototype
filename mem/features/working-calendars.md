name: Working calendars and note clock
description: Site holiday list, note-clock working-day rule, author-out and reschedule rules (Draft values chosen by Mitch)
type: feature
---
- Premier Visalia holidays: US federal + Cesar Chavez Day + day after Thanksgiving; Sat→Fri, Sun→Mon. "Draft — Premier to confirm".
- Org default holiday list copied by each new site, then edited. sys_admin edits sites; coordinator reads.
- Standard notes: 3 of the author's working days (site closed days + author time off skipped). Crisis notes: 24h calendar, never paused.
- "Author out — notes waiting" to coordinator when author out on due day or 2+ working days in a row with open notes.
- New closure/time off over booked visits → "Reschedule needed" for coordinator; never auto-cancel or notify patients.
- Time-off type private; others see only "Out"/"Available".
- Referral chase and county due dates stay calendar days (county cards warn on closed day).
